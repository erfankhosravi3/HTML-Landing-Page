## Control system

Analog control panel (the "ANALOG" in the product name means no digital controller — older revision). Observed:
- Oil pressure gauge
- Battery charger amps gauge (±10 A)
- Rotary engine switch: RUN / START
- Voltage adjust potentiometer
- Engine heater toggle — Caution: do not operate above 0 °F outside
- Danger placard: high voltage behind panel
